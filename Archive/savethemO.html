Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my Premium subscription anytime? ▾
Yes! You can cancel at any time from your plan settings. You'll retain Premium access until the end of your billing period.
Is there a free trial for Premium? ▾
We offer a 7-day free trial when you sign up for a yearly plan. No credit card is charged until the trial ends.
Is my payment information secure? ▾
Absolutely. All payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We never store your card details on our servers.
What happens to my data if I downgrade? ▾
Your data is always safe. If you downgrade, subscriptions beyond the free limit will be read-only and you'll need to delete some to add new ones.
